# Relative time, not temporal hours

A classic sun clock uses temporal hours: Day and Night are each split into 12 equal hours whose length varies with the seasons. We rejected that and tell time as a duration relative to the current Phase (Remaining time or Elapsed time), with Progress shown on the Sun arc. A duration in ordinary hours and minutes answers "how much daylight do I have left?" directly, whereas a temporal hour has to be mentally converted before it means anything.
